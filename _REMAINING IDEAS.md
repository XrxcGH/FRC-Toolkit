**A4. Rounded-hex corner circles are metric stock.** *(motion A6, high confidence.)* `FRC Core.txt:1081-1082`, `RH_500 corner : 0.541 * inch` and `RH_375 corner : 0.404 * inch`. **A5. ## D. Closed — do not raise these again

1. **The sprocket seating curve `0.5025` is correct.** `R = 0.5025 * Dr + 0.0015` is forced by `E = ac + R` with `ac = 0.8 Dr` and `E = 1.3025 Dr + 0.0015`, both of which the source agrees with; `0.50025` would open a `0.00225 Dr` step between seating curve and flank. Documented at `FRC Motion Geomtery.txt:522-528`.
2. **Their #40/#41 roller diameters are swapped.** They list #40 at 0.306 and #41 at 0.3125; B29.1 is #40 = 0.312 and #41 = 0.306 (lightweight). Our 0.312 stands.
3. **Their chain link rounding is worse.** `Chain Path.txt:708` and `Belts.txt:235` round with plain `round()`, so a chain can come out at an odd link count. We round up to even; an odd count needs an offset link, the weakest part of the run. Do not regress it.
4. **Their belt and chain centre-distance approximations are worse.** Both invert the series `L = 2C + PI(D1+D2)/2 + (D2-D1)^2/(4C)` in closed form; our `frcWrapLength` + `frcSolveCenter` bisection is exact. `AV`'s `6.28` for `2*PI` alone costs ~0.05% on centre distance.
5. **Their pulley PLD is wrong; ours is right.** `1.144 mm` is hardcoded for both profiles, so their GT2 3 mm pulley lands 0.382 mm undersize, and their own `beltDef` carries the correct 0.381 and never reads it. Their 0.572 does independently confirm our HTD 5 `pld`; the table stays.
6. **VRM and PCM hole diameter stays 0.150 in.** Electronics Mounting says 0.1695; ours is cited to the CTRE drawings (`FRC Electrical Geometry.txt:118, 126`). Their PDP, PDH, PH and roboRIO figures match ours exactly — good corroboration; these two are the only disagreements and only ours carries a citation. Their radio is the obsolete OM5P.
7. **VersaPlanetary is not getting a 0.1695 in hole** — it is smaller than the #10 screw that has to pass through it. A VersaPlanetary mounts on the CIM-class 2.000 in circle with #10 clearance, which `FRCCotsPattern.CIM_FACE` already provides.
8. **Spark MAX has no mounting holes.** Theirs invents four at 1.732 x 1.311 in; REV-11-2158 shows cable-tie stations only, which is what we model.
9. **`verifyVariableName` does not exist in FS 3044** at either arity (verified). Use `match(n, "^[a-zA-Z_][a-zA-Z0-9_]*$").hasMatch`.
10. **`defineLookupTable` does not exist.** Tables are readable, not authorable — do not plan a feature on authoring one. `getLookupTable` against Onshape's own tables is the only route.
11. **No MAXSpline or SplineXL vendor tooth geometry exists in any of the 52 sources.** Both files pull the profile by `newInstantiator` from a foreign document. Our closed-form arc data (`FRC Core.txt:1087` — rootHalf 8.1829 deg, fillet 0.08071 in, lobe 0.08071 in) remains the only numeric source anywhere.
12. **The half-inch row rule is confirmed, not changed.** `WCD.txt holeOffset()` opens `var holes = length * 2 - 1;` — an independent party on the same rule. Their centring arithmetic afterwards is convoluted; ours is cleaner.
13. **The misspelled-annotation-key sweep was run and the library is clean.** Every annotation key across the eleven tabs is one of `Name`, `Description`, `Default`, `Filter`, `UIHint`, `MaxNumberOfPicks`, `Feature Type Name`, `Feature Name Template`. No `"Collapsed By Defualt"` class of typo exists — though note there are no annotation groups at all, which is a UI observation rather than a defect.
